Have you seen the difference
between the me of years ago
and the me now?
No,look closer!!
Not the smile, not the stride,
look deeper…
to the marrow of meaning,
to the place where identity whispers before it speaks.

Because I have seen it.
The shift,
The shedding,
The slow, sacred undoing
of everything I thought I was.
I was noise before,
echoing expectations,
dressed in borrowed definitions,
calling survival “living.”

But now?
Now I have come into a knowing
not loud, not forced,but steady…
like truth that doesn’t beg to be believed.
I know who I am!
A child of God.
Not by effort,not by merit,
But by mercy that found me
before I ever found myself.

So talk cross....
Talk the weight of wood on wounded shoulders,
talk nails that didn’t just pierce skin but tore the veil between me and grace.

Talk Jesus...
Talk love stretched wide
until it bled forgiveness into a broken world,
Talk a King who wore thorns
so I could wear identity.

Talk burial...
Talk silence,
Talk the stillness that felt like defeat,
the grave that thought it had the final say but didn’t realize
it was only holding a borrowed body.

Talk Jesus,
Because even in death,
He was rewriting endings.
And then?

talk resurrection,
Talk the morning that shattered mourning,
talk stone rolled away like fear losing its grip,
talk life rising not just for Him,
but for me.

Because resurrection didn’t stop at the tomb,
It reached into me.
Breathed into my dry places,
called my name out of dead versions of myself and said,
“Come forth.”

So talk Jesus.
In my becoming.
In my breaking.
In my blooming.
Talk Jesus
in the way I walk now
not perfect, but purposed.
Talk Jesus
in the way I rise,
not untouched, but transformed.
Talk Jesus…
and then talk me.

Because the difference you see?
That gap between who I was
and who I am becoming?
That is what grace looks like
when it finds a home
in a human heart.
