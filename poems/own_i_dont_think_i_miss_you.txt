I don’t think I miss you,
not the way people mean it,
not the aching, empty-chair kind.

I miss…
the way my day leaned toward you,
like sunlight finding a window without trying.

I miss the ordinary things
how “good morning” felt like a ritual,
how silence never stayed long between us,
how laughter came easy,
like it knew the way home.

But somewhere along the line,
I became a quiet notification,
read… then answered later,
then not at all.

And you,
you found louder rooms,
fuller circles,
names that sit closer to your tongue than mine.

It’s strange,
to still carry someone
who has already set you down.

I don’t hate you for it.
I just wish I knew
when I started fading.

So no,
maybe I don’t miss you.
I miss who I was
when I was certain
I mattered to you.
